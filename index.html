Questions people ask

Is it really free?+

Yes. Hoi Plofje is free to use: no trial period, no credit card, no hidden tier.

Who is Plofje?+

Plofje is the AI coach built into the app. It guides your daily practice, gives you feedback on your speaking and writing, and tracks what you should work on next.

Is Plofje a real teacher?+

No, Plofje is AI, not a human teacher. It's built for daily practice and works well alongside a real course, lessons, or Inburgering prep.

Which levels does it cover?+

Hoi Plofje covers A1 through B1, from your first words to the level most people need for everyday life and Inburgering.

Is my level official?+

Your level is an estimate based on your level check and ongoing practice. It's a helpful guide, not an official certificate.

Do I need to download anything?+

No, Hoi Plofje runs right in your browser, on your phone or your laptop.

Can I practice with friends?+

Yes, add friends, see each other's streaks, and join shared challenges to keep each other motivated.

About Hoi Plofje

Built by an expat who needed it too.

Hoi Plofje started with one newcomer trying to learn Dutch, and not finding the right way to keep at it.

When I moved to the Netherlands, I knew Dutch would make everyday life easier: reading letters from the gemeente, chatting with my neighbours, keeping up with the small talk at work. What I couldn't find was the right tool to practice, and to actually keep going, day after day.

In another part of my life, one thing had made a real difference: a personal coach for my fitness. Someone who knows where you are, tells you what to work on next and cheers you on when you show up. I kept wishing I had that for my Dutch.

I wanted a coach for my Dutch, the way I have one for my fitness. So I built one.

A private tutor is one way to get that kind of help. But lessons add up quickly, and a lot of newcomers here simply can't pay for one. So I built my own coach: one that's there whenever you have a few minutes, and that anyone can use.

That coach became Plofje.

Anna, founder of Hoi Plofje
